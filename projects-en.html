Current project(s)

Sexual coercion in baboons (Oct. 2026 - ) ISEM, Montpellier

I am currently working under the supervision of Julia Kunz and Élise Huchard for my first internship of the year. We are exploring the relationship between extended periods of targeted agression between male and female baboons (Papio ursinus), and the social isolation it entails for females. To this end, I will be analyzing the behavioural data collected through the Tsaobis Baboon Project , which has been following troops of chacma baboons in Namibia.

Past project(s)

Resource rivalry and human sex-biased power (Jan. - May 2026) Institut Jean Nicod, Paris

I worked on this project for my second year of masters under the supervision of Jean-Baptiste André and Nicolas Baumard. During this internship, we sought to understand the relationship between resource type,the inequalities it entails, and sex-biased power in human. To this end, I produced an analytical model of human reproductive strategies including resource rivalry, parental allocation, and marriage systems.

The final aim of this project —which I seek to complete next year during my PhD— is to produce an evolutionarily-minded model of human patriarchy by integrating it with evolutionary contractualist theory of morality.

Brinkmanship in biology (Feb. - June 2025) School of Biological Sciences, University of Bristol

I worked on this project for my first year of masters under the supervision of Dr. Patrick Kennedy in the ‘Social Strategy Lab’. We focused on brinkmanship, a strategy which consists in manipulating risk to deter an opponent, and which might well apply to a range of biological contexts. Since brinkmanship was well theorized by political scientists, we aimed at adapting the existing models to a behavioural ecology framework, and see if such a strategy could evolve from the blind process of natural selection.
