Past project(s)

Resource rivalry and human sex-biased power (Jan. - May 2026) Institut Jean Nicod, Paris I worked on this project for my second year of masters under the supervision of Jean-Baptiste André and Nicolas Baumard. During this internship, we sought to find eco-evolutionary explanation for the differences of sex-biased power across human societies and modes of production. To this end, I produced an analytical model of human reproductive strategies including resource rivalry, parental allocation, and marriage systems. The final aim of this project —which I seek to complete next year during my PhD— is to produce an evolutionarily-minding model of human patriarchy by integrating it with evolutionary contractualist theory of morality. Brinkmanship in biology (Feb. - June 2025) School of Biological Sciences, University of Bristol I worked on this project for my first year of masters under the supervision of Dr. Patrick Kennedy in the ‘Social Strategy Lab’.
